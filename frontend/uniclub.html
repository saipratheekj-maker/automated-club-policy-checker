➕ Add a New Policy Rule
Note — How to write a rule sentence Write a simple English sentence that describes a club requirement. Use clear keywords such as "must have", "at least", "must not exceed", or "must be". The system will automatically convert it into a formal policy rule and show it to you for confirmation before saving.
Examples of valid rule sentences
A club must have at least 15 members
A club must have at least 3 meetings per month
Female members must be at least 30 percent
A club must have a faculty advisor
A club must have a constitution
A club must have a treasurer
President must be Junior or Senior
Sports clubs must have at least 20 members
Female members must be at least 40 percent
A club must have at least 5 meetings per month
